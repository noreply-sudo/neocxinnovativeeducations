Nursing Officer Recruitment Common Eligibility Test (NORCET) 10

Notice No. 17/2026 | Date: 24.02.2026

Online Application is invited for the Nursing Officer Recruitment Common Eligibility Test (NORCET) 10 for the recruitment of Nursing Officer posts at Level 07 in the Pay Matrix pre-revised Pay Band-2 of Rs. 9300-34800 with Grade Pay of Rs. 4600/-, Group-B for AIIMS New Delhi and other AIIMS as per available vacancies in the respective Institutes.

Application Timeline

Apply online from 24.02.2026 to 16.03.2026 up to 5:00 P.M.

Official AIIMS Website

Eligibility Criteria for NORCET 10

I
B.Sc. Nursing Pathway
  • a. B.Sc. (Hons.) Nursing / B.Sc. Nursing from an Indian Nursing Council/state Nursing council recognized Institute or University
    OR B.Sc. (Post-Certificate) / Post-Basic B.Sc. Nursing from an INC/State Nursing council recognized Institute/ University.
  • b. Registered as Nurses & Midwife with State / Indian Nursing Council.
II
Diploma (GNM) Pathway
  • a. Diploma in General Nursing Midwifery from an INC/State Nursing council recognized Institute / Board or Council.
  • b. Registered as Nurses & Midwife in State/Indian Nursing Council.
  • c. Two Years' Experience in a minimum 50 bedded Hospital after acquiring the educational qualification.

Remarks: The required two years of experience is an essential criterion and must be acquired after obtaining Essential Qualification.

Age Limit

For all AIIMS: Between 18-30 Years.

(As per detail on Age relaxation given in General Conditions. All age shall be counted on the last date of closing of application form.)

Application Fees

  • General/OBC Rs. 3000/-
  • SC/ST/EWS Rs. 2400/-
  • PwD Candidates Exempted
